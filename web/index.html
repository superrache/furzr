About this app

furzr offers an original way to explore the world of music, browsing relationships between artists or bands and listening samples. This app lets you expand your musical knowledge like the leaves of a tree and find out what you will be listening soon.

Type an artist, then click on any name to expand the map. Drag names around, scroll or pinch to zoom.

Snake (Crazy Snake) keeps only the path you followed: unexplored names vanish at each step. Tree (Huge Tree of Death) removes nothing and lets the map grow forever.

Three modes: Similar artists, Influences (who inspired an artist, and whom they inspired) and, when available, Movies (recommended films).

furzr uses data from Last.fm, Wikidata and TMDB, and audio previews from Deezer. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
